The source uses the journal's numeric mathematics and physics style. All
figures are embedded in the manuscript source, and every bibliography entry
is cited in first-appearance order.

This study is a hardware-aware simulation study. It does not report execution
on a physical quantum processing unit and does not claim quantum advantage.
